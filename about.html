ABOUT

Mechatronics, all the way down.

I'm building the future of space and defense tech: supersonic flight, autonomous systems, and whatever comes after them.

I've worked with NASA, built mission simulations, run computational research on grain boundaries, assembled CubeSats, and programmed robotic arms for lab work. The thread through all of it is the same: I want to understand a system from the physics up, then build the hardware that proves it.

This fall I started at Carnegie Mellon in Mechanical Engineering with a planned minor in Robotics. I'm still in the lab, still on the telescope, and still shipping. There is more detail than fits here on LinkedIn.
